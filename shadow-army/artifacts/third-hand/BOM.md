## Print parameters
PETG; 0.20 mm layer; 4 walls; 5 top/bottom layers; 35% gyroid infill. Print base flat. Print arms flat. Post vertical if adhesion is reliable, otherwise horizontal with supports at pivot bore.

## Assembly
1. Fix post into the Ø18.5 mm base socket; secure mechanically or with M4 transverse fastener after fit check.
2. Connect first arm to post using M5×30 bolt, two washers and nyloc nut.
3. Connect second arm to first arm in the same way.
4. Attach jaw at the terminal M5 pivot.
5. Fit heat-resistant silicone to contact surfaces.
6. Tighten nyloc nuts until joints hold position while remaining manually adjustable.

No physical load test is claimed by this document; 500 g is the design target and requires real-world validation after printing.
